# Rewriting the resume

How the content of the copied canonical is rewritten for one posting. Reached from `skills/tailor-resume/SKILL.md` step 4.

Inputs: the copy in `tailored-resume/`, `posting.md` (`## Brief`, `## Posting`, `## Match`, `## Form` when it exists), `DATA_DIR/profile.md`.

Sentence-level rules — length, openings, banned turns of phrase, the line-by-line review — live in `shared/references/writing-rules.md` and apply to every line written here.

## Summary block

Two or three sentences positioning the candidate for this role, not for the trade in general. Lead with the credential this posting cares about most: the years, the biggest result, or the closest company. Place two or three keywords of `## Brief` as the posting writes them. End on what makes this candidate distinct from the other applicants.

## Experience

One bullet = one action verb, what was done, at what scale, the measured result.

- The first two bullets of each role are the ones this posting asks for; the rest follow by decreasing relevance.
- Figures come from `profile.md` `## Metrics bank`, each attached to the role it belongs to.
- A bullet the posting never touches leaves the resume rather than sitting as noise.
- A bullet the canonical never carried, held by `profile.md` and asked for here, joins the role it belongs to.
- Titles, dates and the list of roles stay exactly as the canonical has them. Every role stays, gaps included.

Bullet count: five to seven for the current role, three to five for the previous ones, two or three for roles more than five years old.

## Skills

Reordered to open with what the posting emphasises, grouped the way the posting frames them. Skills this role never touches leave the list.

## Level calibration

- Executive: strategy, organisational design, board-level communication, business impact rather than task completion.
- Director: programme ownership, team building, cross-functional leadership, operational excellence; upward alignment and downward development both visible.
- Senior individual contributor or manager: hands-on depth, concrete deliverables, mentoring, influence without authority.

## Strict accuracy

- Only what the canonical resume, `profile.md`, or a correction from the candidate states explicitly. A hole stays a hole.
- Business model stays unnamed unless the source names it: describe what the company does instead of labelling it B2B, B2C, SaaS or marketplace.
- Scope stays as written: "revenue targets" stays revenue targets, never "P&L ownership"; a named function is never widened into a neighbouring one.
- Partners stay as listed: the source names Marketing and Sales, the bullet names Marketing and Sales.
- Reframing works on what exists — reorder, rewrite, mirror the posting's words where they are true. Every claim traces back to one fact in one source.
- Ambiguity gets conservative wording, or the detail is dropped.

## Before returning

- Each line of `## Match` marked `direct` is visible somewhere in the resume.
- Keywords of the posting appear where they are true, and nowhere else.
- Each figure sits in the role that produced it.
- Titles, dates and the roster of roles are those of the canonical.
- Action verbs vary; the language sits at the seniority of the target role.
