# Writing the letter

How `cover-letter.md` is composed. Reached from `skills/cover-letter/SKILL.md` step 2.

Inputs: `posting.md` `## Brief` and `## Match`, the tailored resume in `tailored-resume/`, `profile.md` `## Letter rules` and `## Metrics bank`.

Sentence-level rules — length, openings, banned turns of phrase, tense of a finished role, the line-by-line review — live in `shared/references/writing-rules.md` and apply to every line written here.

## Proof map

Written first, above the letter. Take from `## Match` the requirements this letter will answer, one line each, in the same shape:

```
requirement → evidence → source → quality (direct | analogous | transferable)
```

Keep the lines the letter actually uses, plus every must-have of the posting. A must-have with no evidence, not even transferable, is written with its hole named: the application goes to `to validate` and the letter claims nothing in its place.

## Letter

Language of the posting. 150 to 250 words, unless `preferences.md` `## Application rules` sets another band.

1. **Hook**, one or two sentences: what the company does, named concretely, and why this role. The keywords of `## Brief` appear here as the posting writes them.
2. **Proof**, two or three quantified results that answer the responsibilities of `## Brief`. Each result names the role that produced it, with the figures of `## Metrics bank`. They are the same evidence the tailored resume carries, told as short stories rather than bullets.
3. **Close**, two or three sentences proposing the conversation. Availability, notice and location belong to the form fields, not to the closing.

The candidate's own narrative rules — what to say first, what to keep to a single sentence, how a company or a client is named — are in `profile.md` `## Letter rules` and override the shape above.

## Before returning

- Word count inside the band.
- Every sentence reviewed line by line per `shared/references/writing-rules.md`.
- Each named company carries only the results filed under its own role and dates.
- Chronology checked against the resume before any "earlier" or "later".
- Each proof answers a requirement that appears in the proof map.
