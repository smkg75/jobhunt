# Preferences

## Target roles
_The job titles worth reading, in the words the boards use._

## Location
_Where the job can be: cities, remote, hybrid, how many onsite days are acceptable._

## Compensation
_The floor and the target, with the currency and the period; gross or net stated._

## Sectors
_Sectors sought, and sectors merely accepted._

## Company size
_The size range that fits, in headcount or in stage._

## Must-haves
_One line per requirement a posting has to meet to go further._

## Nice-to-haves
_One line per thing that lifts a posting._

## Dealbreakers
_One line per hard exclusion._

## Freshness
_How old a posting may be and still be worth reading._

## Send mode
_`draft` — everything filled, nothing submitted. `auto-submit` — submitted without asking._

## Application rules
_What overrides the skills' defaults: cover letter length, what to write and what to leave to the
form, the numeric bar the company itself has to clear._

## Sources & accounts
_Boards and services the candidate holds an account on, collectives and agencies registered with, and
what each one is used for._

## Query rules
_How search queries are phrased: languages, words that work, words that pull in noise._
